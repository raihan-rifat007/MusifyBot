Telegram Bot

Type a song name.
Get the track back.

A Telegram bot for searching and downloading music, with no-slash shortcuts and a companion mini app.

you: play perfect ed sheeran

bot: [downloads instantly, sends the audio file]

you: search shape of you

bot: 🔍 Searching shape of you on the music library...

bot: Results for "shape of you" — 1-10 of 14. Tap a track, or reply with its number.

you: 3

bot: [downloads and sends that track]

What it does

Play instantly
/play, or just type play <song> or p <song> — downloads the top match immediately, no list.
Search a pick-list
/search, or search <song> or find <song> — shows numbered results to choose from.
Lyrics lookup
Pull lyrics for any track by artist and title, or straight from a track you just played.
10 results per page
Numbered, tappable results with page-forward, page-back, and a delete-list button.
Companion mini app
A brutalist in-Telegram app for searching without typing commands.
Real filenames
Every file arrives named by artist and title — never a raw hash.

How it works

Telegram delivers updates to this server over a webhook. Playing a track searches the API and downloads the top match straight into memory; searching sends a "Searching..." message that edits in place into a numbered, tappable list once results arrive. Either way, the finished file is uploaded to Telegram with real title and artist metadata.
